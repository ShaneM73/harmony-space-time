How to read this map

Each marker
One composer or artist with at least five analysed pieces (136 in all), placed by the average harmony of their pieces.
Axes · factor analysis
Colour = harmonic group
Six clusters from Ward clustering on all 14 harmonic measures. The groups were found on all 14 measures, not on these three axes, so a colour can look a little scattered here.
Shape = genre
● classical■ jazz▲ rock & pop
Size = pieces analysed
Grows with the logarithm of the number of pieces in the corpora: 561 pieces (Bach) is about 2.3× the width of 5. It shows coverage, not importance; bigger markers are more reliable averages. Turn off “Size by pieces” for equal sizes.
52060200561
Faded = border member
Stays with its group in under half of 300 bootstrap re-runs of the clustering (e.g. Monteverdi, Schütz, Marvin Gaye).
Lines and labels
Thin lines drop each marker to the floor to show depth. Chips mark each group’s centre. Names show for composers with 15+ pieces; hover or click for any other.
