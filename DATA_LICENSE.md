- When in Rome — CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://github.com/MarkGotham/When-in-Rome
- Rock Corpus (de Clercq & Temperley) — https://rockcorpus.midside.com/
- McGill Billboard (Burgoyne, Wild & Fujinaga) — https://ddmal.music.mcgill.ca/research/The_McGill_Billboard_Project_(Chord_Analysis_Dataset)/
- Weimar Jazz Database (Pfleiderer et al.) — https://jazzomat.hfm-weimar.de/dbformat/dboverview.html
- all modern corpora obtained through ChoCo — https://github.com/smashub/choco

Location assignments in `analysis/locations.py` are typed from standard biographical reference and carry a confidence label per piece.
